# Product vision

RunFloor gives every sales representative a reliable, company-specific guide for product knowledge, customer conversations, objection handling, and follow-up. It helps a representative move from uncertainty to a useful next action without replacing their judgment or relationship with the customer.

## Version 1 users

- **Company administrator:** maintains company details, products, approved knowledge, sales content, and team access.
- **Sales representative:** asks questions, researches products, prepares for objections, and drafts customer follow-up.

## Version 1 promise

A company can organize its approved sales knowledge in one place and make it immediately useful to its team through a simple, guided workspace.

## Product principles

1. Approved company information is the source of truth.
2. Guidance should be clear, practical, and respectful of the customer.
3. The system should ask useful discovery questions before recommending a close.
4. Tenant data must remain private and isolated.
5. Administrators must be able to understand and control what the assistant uses.
6. Automation supports the representative; it does not impersonate them or make unsupported claims.

## Version 1 success signals

- Representatives can find an approved answer faster than searching scattered documents.
- Draft messages require little editing and remain factually grounded.
- Administrators can identify missing or outdated knowledge.
- Teams return to the product during real sales conversations.

## Not in Version 1

CRM automation, autonomous outreach, AI-generated coaching evaluations, executive analytics, operations workflows, billing, and marketplace integrations are future considerations. Version 1 may use transparent deterministic coaching scores for structured practice, but those scores must not be represented as AI evaluation.
